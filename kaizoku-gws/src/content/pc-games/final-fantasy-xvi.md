## About

Final Fantasy XVI is an action role-playing game developed and published by Square Enix. The sixteenth main installment in the Final Fantasy series, the game is set in the world of Valisthea, a land divided among six nations that each harness the power of mystical Eikons. The story follows Clive Rosfield, the first son of the Grand Duchy of Rosaria, as he embarks on a dark journey of revenge and redemption spanning decades. The game features fast-paced real-time combat, epic Eikon battles, and a mature narrative.
