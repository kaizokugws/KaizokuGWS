## About

Rise of the Ronin is an open-world action role-playing game developed by Team Ninja and published by Sony Interactive Entertainment. Set during the Bakumatsu period in late 19th-century Japan, the story follows a nameless ronin — a masterless samurai — who must navigate the turbulent political landscape between the Tokugawa shogunate and anti-shogunate factions. The game features deep combat mechanics, a vast open world to explore, and a branching narrative shaped by player choices.
