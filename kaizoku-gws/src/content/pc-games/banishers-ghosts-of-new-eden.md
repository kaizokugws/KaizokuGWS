## About

Banishers: Ghosts of New Eden is an action role-playing game developed by Don't Nod and published by Focus Entertainment. Set in an alternate 17th-century New England, players control Antea Duarte and Red mac Raith, a pair of ghost hunters known as Banishers. Tasked with lifting a curse from the land, they must decide the fates of the ghosts they encounter, choosing to either help the living or side with the dead. The game blends action combat with narrative-driven choices and exploration.
