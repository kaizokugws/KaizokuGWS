## System Requirements

### Minimum

- CPU: Intel Core i5-9600K (3.7 GHz) or AMD Ryzen 5 3600 (3.60 GHz)
- RAM: 8 GB
- Video Card: NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1660 Ti or AMD Radeon RX 590
- OS: Windows 10 64-bit
- Free Disk Space: 45 GB

- CPU: Intel Core i7-10700K (3.8 GHz) or AMD Ryzen 5 5600X (3.7 GHz)
- RAM: 16 GB
- Video Card: NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2070 or AMD Radeon RX 5700 XT
- OS: Windows 10 64-bit
