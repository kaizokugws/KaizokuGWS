## About

CarX Street is a high-fidelity, open-world racing simulator developed by CarX Technologies, LLC that serves as a spiritual successor to the CarX Drift Racing series. The game is built on the proprietary CarX Technology physics engine, emphasizing realistic weight transfer, tire friction, and aerodynamic downforce to create a complex mechanical sandbox for vehicle tuning and racing.

The game is set in Sunset City, a fictional metropolis inspired by the styles of Los Angeles, San Francisco, Miami, and Tokyo, featuring diverse environments such as urban downtown districts, industrial zones, and treacherous mountain "touge" passes. Players progress by joining racing clubs, defeating boss characters like Roxi Foxy and Mac Tennessee, and participating in various events including street racing, sprints, and drifting within a persistent online world.
